Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

EZ is an early-access product testing network run by Vivan, an individual based in India. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google or GitHub we receive your name, email address, profile picture and your account ID from that provider. We never receive your password.
  • What you create in EZ. Missions you post (company name, product link, descriptions), your credit balance and the missions you have completed.
  • Feedback. Missions link to feedback forms (for example Google Forms). What you type there goes to the form owner through Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Technical data. Our hosting providers may log your IP address and browser type to keep the service running.

How we use it

To sign you in, show your name and photo, run missions and credits, contact you about your feedback or credits, and protect EZ from abuse. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we use your name, email address and profile picture only to create your account and sign you in. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it except with the service providers listed below that run EZ.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run EZ: Supabase (sign-in and account data), GitHub (website hosting and GitHub sign-in) and Google (Google sign-in and Google Forms). Founders can see the feedback you submit for their mission, including your email if you enter it. We may disclose information if the law requires it. Some of these providers store data on servers outside India.

Local storage

EZ uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, to keep you signed in and to remember your missions, credits and choices on your device. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw your consent, and make a complaint. Email YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

EZ is not for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continuing to use EZ means you accept the updated policy.
