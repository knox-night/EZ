Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

EZ is an early-access prototype for product testing, operated by Vivan in India. This policy describes information handled by the current prototype and the choices available to you.

Information handled when you sign in

If you sign in with Google or GitHub, Supabase Auth receives account information from that provider. EZ uses the signed-in account ID and available profile information, such as your name, email address and profile picture, to display your account. EZ does not receive your provider password.

Mission previews and feedback

The example missions are included in the website code. A mission preview you create is kept temporarily in the current page's memory and is cleared when you reload the page or change accounts. EZ does not currently save mission previews, tester profiles, applications or feedback to an EZ database. EZ does not currently provide founder review or credit awards.

If you open a product or feedback-form link, you leave EZ. Information you submit on that product or form goes to its operator, not to an EZ submission system, and is covered by that operator's policies.

How information is used and shared

Account information is used to sign you in and show your account name and picture. Authentication is provided by Supabase and the identity provider you choose. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies. EZ does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Browser storage

The Supabase client stores your authentication session in browser storage so you can remain signed in. EZ does not use browser storage as an authoritative source for missions or credits; mission previews are temporary page data. You can sign out or clear your browser storage to remove the local sign-in session.

Retention and your choices

EZ does not currently have an application database for mission, profile or feedback data. Supabase Auth handles account records and session data under the project's configuration and retention settings. You can ask to access, correct or delete personal data, withdraw consent, or make a complaint under applicable law by emailing sigmaboy789265@gmail.com. Account deletion may require action in the Supabase project; EZ does not currently provide a self-service deletion control.

Children

EZ is intended for adults aged 18 or over.
